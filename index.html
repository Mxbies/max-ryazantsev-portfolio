CASE / 02

ДИАСТОП

ADVERTISING / SCRIPT / VIDEO CONCEPT

A 60-second vertical advertising concept for a Moscow podology center for people with diabetes.

During my internship at ООО «Диастоп», I developed the creative concept and script for the video. The structure moves from problem and risk to professional care and a call to action.

00–07

ЖЁСТКАЯ ПРАВДА — opening with the problem and emotional tension.

07–15

15–25

ВАШ ВЫБОР — the viewer faces a decision.

25–40

ДИАСТОП — professional care and expertise.

40–55

ЧТО ВЫ ПОЛУЧАЕТЕ — reassurance and confidence.

55–60

ФИНАЛ — concise call to action.
